# Where the guard actually was — 30 September 2026

From `scene.scn` (X32), `Session-stp.uadmix` (UA Console), the Loopback screenshot,
and CoreAudio's own channel table for the Apollo this Mac is driving. The X32 does
not need to change. One setting in UA Console does.

## The two paths, as wired today

```
THE LOOP (raw — the guard is not in it)

  LEAD mic ─► X32 preamp 3 (+30 dB) ─► X-ADAT card out 3 ─► Apollo ADAT in 3
      ─► Console: "ADAT 3 → Output Destination: ADAT 3"  (direct, −0.03 dB)
      ─► Apollo ADAT out 3 ─► X32 card in 3 ─► ch03 "LEAD" ─► Leader bus ─► monitors ─► mic


THE GUARD (a side branch that gets ADDED to the loop)

  Apollo ADAT in 3 ─► engine (input 4) ─► VIRTUAL 1 (output 10 — software, never leaves the box)
      ─► Console VIRTUAL 1 (−11 dB)  +  Loopback: Chrome, Music, Pass-Thru (same pair)
      ─► Apollo ADAT out 1 ─► X32 card in 1 ─► ch01  (no name, fader −44.8 dB,
                                                       pre-fader send −0.3 dB into Leader)
```

ch01/ch02 are the unnamed stereo pair at the far left of the desk with their faders
nearly off. Their Leader-bus sends are **pre-fader** (`EQ->`), so the −44.8 does
nothing — they have been feeding the processed vocal into your monitor mix at full
level all along, beside the raw one on ch03. A 45 dB notch in one of two parallel
paths changes the sum by at most 6 dB. That is every "it did nothing" of the last
week.

## What changed (done)

The engine's returns now go to **ADAT out 3 and 4** (outputs 4, 5) instead of
VIRTUAL 1/2. The processed LEAD and BGV therefore land on X32 card in 3/4 — the
same channels the raw copies land on today — so **ch03 LEAD and ch04 BGV carry the
guard's output**, in your strips, with your EQ and trim, no desk changes.

VIRTUAL 1/2 now carry only Loopback (Chrome, Music) → ch01/02 → Leader bus. That is
what those two channels are for.

## What you do (one thing, in UA Console)

For **ADAT 3** and **ADAT 4** in Console:

1. **Output Destination → none** (it is currently set to ADAT 3 / ADAT 4). This is the
   raw passthrough. With it on, card 3 receives raw + processed summed — louder and
   slightly phasey; with it off, card 3 receives the guard alone.
2. **Mute both channels** in Console's mixer. Once the direct-out is cleared they fall
   back into Console's monitor mix, and if your speakers are on the Apollo's MON outs
   that would be a third raw path. A Console mute does not affect what the engine
   receives.

That is the entire change. Nothing on the X32 moves.

## How you know it worked (ten seconds)

The same mute test as before. Mute the guard's output and talk:

- **you go silent in the monitors** → the guard is the only path. Done.
- **you still hear yourself** → a raw path remains; tell me which speakers you are
  on (Apollo MON outs, or X32 XLR outs) and that narrows it to one cable.

Then raise the gain. For the first time, the notch is in the only path there is.
